OUR GOAL

Through Anonymity: Against Censorship

We aim to bring you the uncensored truth, in a world full of censorship. All while maintaining your anonymity and the anonymity of our journalists.

We hope that AnonymiTea can serve you as its shelter, whether this era of censorship will last. We will be around until journalism can be safe again, and will remain long after, to preserve history.

We Value

Your Privacy

Our utmost priority is your privacy and safety. Whether you are a reader who wants to learn the uncensored truth, or a writer whose aim is to bring the truth to the public, we will protect your identity.

Your Contribution

As AnonymiTea is a collection of articles, your contribution and voices are the backbone of our website. While we plan on publishing articles based on fact, we plan to be a host to your personal opinions.

The Truth

Misinformation and censorship are prevalent in this day and age. We aim to bring the truth to you, devoid of any censorship and with as much research to back up our articles.

Integrity

We value the truth, not only from everywhere else, but also from within. We aim to be as transparent as we can, with our readers. As a staple that brings you the truth, it is only right if we stand by it.

Community

We value the community we serve and create articles for. We aim to collaborate and communicate with fellow journalists, so we may be able to publish articles and to get through any difficult times.

Independence

We aim to bring the unbiased, uncensored truth. Which is why we work independently from any group, party, or government.
