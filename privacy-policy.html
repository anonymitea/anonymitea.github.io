PRIVACY POLICY

Your Data

AnonymiTea values the privacy of our journalists and our readers. So, true to what we stand for, we do not collect any personal data from our users or use any cookies. We do not offer user accounts or allow registration and logins.

That unfortunately means that we will not be able to provide some of the services most Journalism websites usually provide. Newsletters, donations, subscriptions, etc. have been disabled.

What Happens When You Submit an Article

As much as we try not to collect any user data, there is a slight exception if you so choose to submit an article for us to publish. Some of your browser data will be collected by our systems when you submit an article.

Your information will remain in our system for a maximum of one hour before being completely erased. We will not keep any of your data for longer than we need to. Do not worry, as none of your information will be sent anywhere or to anyone, and will remain only in our systems before being deleted.

Copyright

Due to the anonymous nature of our website, we will not be able to fully protect our works, and by extension, your works. Especially not through legal action. If you submit an article, you are agreeing to publish your work despite there being a chance that your work may be stolen or reposted elsewhere without credit.

The most we can do to combat the stealing of works, is by claiming that it is article made by AnonymiTea, but we can not state any names, as that defeats the whole purpose of our website.
