The Upcoming Presidential Election and Dowdy’s Endorsement of McGruff

A closer look at the representatives of this election's Republican Party. Should we really vote for Dowdy's pick, McGruff?

October 30, ███4, 12:23 am

A photograph of the Republican Presidential candidate, Paul McGruff taken in Minnesota, ███████████, January 12 ████.

President Kenn Dowdy’s 8 year presidency is coming to a close as November quickly approaches. While the administration has faced a fair share of challenges, the United States has remained resilient throughout.

The presidential race has narrowed down to the three most anticipated candidates: Ronnie Martin representing the Democratic Party, Paul McGruff representing the Republican Party, and Portia Bailey representing the Developmentalist Party.

That brings us to President Dowdy’s endorsement of this year’s Republican Runner, Paul McGruff.

Dowdy claimed in last month’s speech, that “McGruff will prove to be the best prepared to lead the United States for the upcoming years” and that “McGruff has my full support to be the next president.”
But should we take Dowdy’s word for it? What would it mean for us, the citizens of the United States, if McGruff won the election?

First, what has McGruff promised the United States, in the event that he gets elected as president?

In Paul McGruff’s Public Speech, in Minnesota, January 12 ████, McGruff has expressed interest in putting America’s defense first and improving the US’s military funding. “Tensions have been steadily rising between nations, and there is no doubt that we might end up in the crossfire. If I am elected as president, I will ensure that America will have its best possible military, and that we will keep our spot as one of- if not, the strongest country.”

During several of McGruff’s interviews, he also discussed his plan to enforce a tax bill on illegal immigrants. He claimed that he wishes to prioritize the well being of US born citizens and the betterment of the country. “If this bill is passed, then we will be able to give US citizens more affordable healthcare, more affordable living, and a better life.” McGruff also claims that “it is perfect. And it is a fair way to deal with illegal immigrants. Why should we kick them out, when we can use this as an opportunity?”

McGruff has made it a goal for himself, and a promise to the US, that he would better the country’s economy. An ambitious goal, but we’ll never know if McGruff will be able to succeed in his goals or not, until he is president.
As of today, McGruff seems to be the most favored candidate of the three, being in the lead in the polls.
