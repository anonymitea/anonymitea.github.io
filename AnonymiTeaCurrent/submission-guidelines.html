SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

AnonymiTea gives you the opportunity to pitch an article of your own, while guaranteeing your privacy and safety. We act as an outlet for the stories you wish to publish.

The Contents of Your Article

When writing and pitching your article, make sure that you have the following prepared, alongside the manuscript for your article:

Article Subject
State what the subject of your article is. What is the main talking point of your article? Some examples include: Global Warming, Monopoly, Government, Environment, etc.

The Synopsis
Please include a summary of what your article talks about or argues. Keep the synopsis brief and with a clear message.

Sources
Provide any sources to back up the article you are writing, doing so will make our fact-checking process much quicker. You must have permission, or must own any images you wish to have up with your article. Abide by any Copyright Laws in place.

We are going to be thorough when checking through submissions. Any submission that goes against US laws will be immediately denied.

Usage of AI

We do not allow the usage of AI in your works. This includes any writing or image generated by AI. We want to see the authenticity in your report. If it is found that AI was used in any part of your article submission, it will be rejected immediately.
