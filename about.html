ABOUT

Who we are

AnonimiTea is a US-based, nonprofit anonymous article site dedicated to keeping journalism alive. Established October 22, ███4, AnonimiTea exists to combat the wave of censorship that took down several US-based journalism sites.

We believe in keeping people informed, all while maintaining the anonymity of both the readers and contributors alike. And we will do this by providing a platform where people can publish anonymously.

AnonymiTea is run by a small independent team of experienced journalists who have written articles of their own in the past. Journalists who have made it past censorship, and are starting a new, safer place for fellow Journalists.

UNITY AND ANONYMITY

What we cover

The AnonymiTeam will cover a wide range of topics, especially the topics that need to be brought to light more often. We focus mostly on Politics, Environment, and topics that apply in the US.
Writers may also contribute their work and ideas under other topics not listed. This also includes personal opinions.

Dealing with Misinformation

Since anyone is able to submit an article, and their identity will not be tied to their submission, how can we make sure that the information we are putting out is credible?

Every submission will undergo a fact-check verification and an editorial review process, done by the AnonymiTeam before it is published. To speed up the verification process, you may submit: sources, video, or photographic material to back up your report. Any articles that are not able to be fully verified with sources will be labelled as such, so that we may maintain transparency with our readers.

This will not apply for Personal Opinion articles.

How we Make Money

Short answer: We do not. As stated before, AnonymiTea is completely non-profit. AnonymiTea is meant to be accessible, and for the people. No information is locked behind a paywall or subscription. Unfortunately, we also will not be accepting donations, since money and payment services can be tracked down to an individual.
